Important things to note

- The mouse cursor outline shows whether the arm is able to reach the desired target location. A green outline means the location is reachable by the arm, and a red outline means that it is not.

- Setting target locations: The origin of the coordinate system is fixed at the base of the arm. X spans the entire width of the window, ranging -width / at the left of the origin, to width / 2 at the right. Y spans the entire height of the window, ranging from 0 at the origin to the max height of the window.

- To disable arm mouse tracking, press 'toggle mouse tracking' or add target input by entering a real number into the entry or by moving the sliders.
